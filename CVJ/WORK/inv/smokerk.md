## Quality assessment for this talk
- Strong: the only item among the sources in this set that has a CT/MR landmark figure (F1) and a clean panel of craniometry lines on matched radiograph and MR (F2), plus a worked set of pathology cases with measurements (F3-F7, F27). Clear classic definitions: Chamberlain, McGregor, Wackenheim, basal angle, clivus-canal angle, atlanto-occipital joint axis angle (Table 2). Good congenital-anomaly pictorial (atlas arch, os odontoideum vs type II fracture, ossiculum terminale), Down syndrome, Klippel-Feil, achondroplasia, MPS, OI.
- Weak/absent for the PRIORITY list: NO values or definitions for basion-dens interval, basion-axial interval, condyle-C1 interval, Ranawat, Redlund-Johnell, Clark station, McRae line, Welcher (basal angle appears only as "basal angle", credited to McGregor). Powers ratio and basion-axis interval are only named (Down syndrome, p.19) with no formula or normal value. Atlantodental interval: only "normal<5 mm" (AADI, Down syndrome context) and 4.5 mm threshold in a cited study; no adult versus child split. No pediatric norm tables.
- Little on trauma classification, rheumatoid disease, infection/TB, neoplasm, or surgical technique. Surgical management content is limited to flexion-extension assessment, brief remarks (posterior fossa decompression in F4 caption; marrow transplantation reversal in MPS; spontaneous resolution of rotatory subluxation in children).
- Use: congenital/syndromic pathology and Chamberlain/Wackenheim/basal-angle/clivus-canal teaching; take the other measurements from other sources.
