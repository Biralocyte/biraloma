# Chen & Liu 2009 - recommended references (copied exactly from reference list, PDF p.26-28)
1. Smoker WRK. Craniovertebral junction: normal anatomy, craniometry, and congenital anomalies. Radiographics 1994;14:255-77. [ref 3] - the craniometry source the review itself lacks (lines, angles, intervals).
2. Anderson LD, D'Alonzo RT. Fracture of the odontoid process of the axis. J Bone Joint Surg Am 1974;56:1663-74. [ref 21] - original odontoid fracture types I-III.
3. Anderson PA, Montesano PX. Morphology and treatment of occipital condyle fractures. Spine 1988;13(7):731-6. [ref 28] - occipital condyle classification with treatment implications.
4. Traynelis VC, Marano GD, Dunker RO, et al. Traumatic atlanto-occipital dislocation: case report. J Neurosurg 1986;65:863-70. [ref 31] - source of AOD types I-III used in the talk.
5. Teegala R, Kumar P, Kale S, et al. Craniovertebral junction tuberculosis: a new comprehensive therapeutic strategy. Neurosurgery 2008;63:946-55. [ref 58] - the only management-oriented (surgical decision) reference, for CVJ TB.
Also useful: Effendi B, Roy D, Cornish B, et al. Fractures of the ring of the axis. A classification based on the analysis of 131 cases. J Bone Joint Surg Br 1981;63-B(3):319-27. [ref 32]; Holt RG, Helms CA, Munk PL, et al. Hypertrophy of C-1 anterior arch: useful sign to distinguish os odontoideum from acute dens fracture. Radiology 1989;173(1):207-9. [ref 22]
Note: the list is dated (newest cited 2008); no newer classifications.
