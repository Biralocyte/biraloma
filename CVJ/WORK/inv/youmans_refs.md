# Top references from Youmans ch. 260 (copied exactly from reference list)
1. Smoker WR. Craniovertebral junction: normal anatomy, craniometry, and congenital anomalies. Radiographics. 1994;14(2):255–277. -- Radiology-journal source for craniometry lines/values that this chapter only names (ref 48; also Suggested Readings).
2. Machnowska M, Raybaud C. Imaging of the craniovertebral junction anomalies in children. Adv Tech Stand Neurosurg. 2014;40:141–170. -- Pediatric CVJ imaging review (ref 50), paediatric measurements and anomalies.
3. Menezes AH, Fenoy KA. Remnants of occipital vertebrae: proatlas segmentation abnormalities. Neurosurgery. 2009;64(5):945–953. -- Source of Fig. 260.1 and the proatlas classification with 3D-CT/MRI examples.
4. Dlouhy BJ, Policeni BA, Menezes AH. Reduction of atlantoaxial dislocation prevented by pathological position of the transverse ligament in fixed, irreducible os odontoideum: operative illustrations and radiographic correlates in 41 patients. J Neurosurg Spine. 2017;27(1):20–28. -- Imaging correlates predicting irreducibility, directly changes surgical route.
5. Dahdaleh NS, Dlouhy BJ, Menezes AH. Application of neuromuscular blockade and intraoperative 3D imaging in the reduction of basilar invagination. J Neurosurg Pediatr. 2012;9(2):119–124. -- Intraoperative CT-verified reduction changing ventral vs dorsal strategy.
