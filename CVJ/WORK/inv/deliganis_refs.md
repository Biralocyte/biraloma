# deliganis - references worth chasing (numbering as in Deliganis 2000, printed p. S249-S250)

1. [ref 7] Harris JH, Carson GC, Wagner LK. Radiologic diagnosis of traumatic occipitovertebral dissociation. 1. Normal occipitovertebral relationships on lateral radiographs of supine subjects. AJR Am J Roentgenol 1994; 162:881-886. - source of the basion-dens and basion-posterior axial line normal values (adults and children) quoted in Table 3; needed to cite the primary cut-offs.
2. [ref 8] Harris JH, Carson GC, Wagner LK, Kerr N. Radiologic diagnosis of traumatic occipitovertebral dissociation. 2. Comparison of three methods of detecting occipitovertebral relationships on lateral radiographs of supine subjects. AJR Am J Roentgenol 1994; 162:887-892. - per its title compares three detection methods; likely where Powers ratio/BDI-BAI performance is compared (the Deliganis file does not give Powers ratio).
3. [ref 13] Anderson PA, Montesano PX. Morphology and treatment of occipital condyle fractures. Spine 1988; 13:731-736. - cited for the impaction / extension of skull fracture / avulsion condyle fracture types, and its title includes treatment (the surgical-management angle missing from Deliganis).
