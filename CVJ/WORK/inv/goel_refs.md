# goel - references worth chasing (numbering as in Goel 2009 reference list, printed p. 246-247)

1. Goel A. Treatment of basilar invagination by atlantoaxial joint distraction and direct lateral mass fixation. J Neurosurg Spine 2004;1:281-6. [ref 12] - cited as the source of the Group A/B classification and the facet distraction/fixation technique.
2. Goel A, Bhatjiwale M, Desai K. Basilar invagination: a study based on 190 surgically treated cases. J Neurosurg 1998;88:962-8. [ref 11] - original Group I/II (Chiari-based) classification, modified omega angle, brainstem girth, posterior fossa volume.
3. Goel A, Shah A. Reversal of longstanding musculoskeletal changes in basilar invagination after surgical decompression and stabilization. J Neurosurg Spine 2009;10:220-7. [ref 1] - 170-patient series supporting reversibility of short neck, torticollis, platybasia, craniospinal angulation (instability-driven concept).
